Hospitality Industry Analytics
📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes hotel booking data to understand booking trends, customer behavior, cancellation patterns, length of stay, distribution channels, and revenue-related factors.

The main objective is to use Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) and data visualization to identify meaningful patterns and provide business insights that can help hotels improve their decision-making.

🎯 Objectives
Analyze hotel booking patterns
Understand customer behavior
Analyze cancellation trends
Study booking lead time
Analyze length of stay
Understand distribution channels
Compare different customer and market segments
Identify important patterns using data visualization
Provide actionable business recommendations

🔍 Key Insights
🏨 Distribution Channel

The analysis shows that TA/TO (Travel Agencies/Tour Operators) is the dominant distribution channel, accounting for approximately 82% of bookings.

Direct bookings account for around 12.3%, while corporate bookings represent approximately 5.6%.

Business recommendation:
The hotel could increase direct bookings through loyalty benefits, exclusive website offers, and direct-booking packages to reduce dependence on intermediaries.

📅 Lead Time

The lead-time distribution is strongly right-skewed. Most bookings have relatively short lead times, while a smaller number of customers make reservations several months in advance.

Business recommendation:
The hotel can use early-booking promotions to encourage advance reservations and use demand-based pricing for customers booking closer to their arrival date.

🛏️ Length of Stay

The analysis indicates that the average guest stay is generally around 3–4 nights across arrival dates.

Business recommendation:
The hotel could introduce long-stay packages, discounts, or additional benefits to encourage guests to extend their stay.

💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the hotel could:

Increase direct bookings through exclusive offers.
Use dynamic pricing based on booking demand and lead time.
Introduce incentives for guests who extend their stay.
Analyze cancellation behavior by lead time and customer segment.
Focus marketing efforts on high-performing market segments.
Monitor distribution-channel performance based on both booking volume and revenue.

📌 Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Python-based data analysis and visualization can be used to convert hotel booking data into meaningful business insights.

The analysis helps identify patterns in customer behavior, booking channels, lead time, cancellations, and length of stay, which can support better revenue management, marketing strategies, and hotel operations.
